1. Introduction

This policy covers bytecraftsoftware.com, the website of Byte Craft Software, based in Chesterfield, Michigan, USA. It is a brochure site describing what we do and how to reach us.

Our individual products each have their own privacy policy covering the data they hold. This policy does not apply to them — see Our Products.

2. What We Collect

You can read every page of this site without giving us anything. We collect information in only two circumstances:

  • If you contact us. The name, email address, and message you type into the contact form, or that you send us by email directly. The message we receive also notes when it was sent, your browser's user-agent string, and the country the request came from, which we use to tell genuine enquiries from automated spam.
  • Ordinary web analytics. Which pages are visited, roughly where in the world the visit came from, what kind of device and browser, and how the page is scrolled and clicked. See Cookies & Analytics.

We do not ask for, and this site has no way to accept, payment details or account credentials.

3. How We Use It

  • To reply to you when you get in touch.
  • To understand which parts of the site are useful and which are confusing, so we can improve it.
  • To keep the site working and to investigate faults or abuse.

We do not sell your information, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not use it to build an advertising profile of you.

4. Cookies & Analytics

This site uses Microsoft Clarity to understand how it is used: which pages people visit, how far they scroll, where they click, and where they get stuck. Clarity can replay an anonymised recording of a visit so we can see a problem rather than guess at it. It sets cookies in order to recognise a returning visit.

Clarity masks text content in its recordings by default.

We do not use advertising trackers or third-party marketing cookies. This site sets no cookies of its own — there is nothing to sign in to.

5. Third-Party Services

This site relies on the following providers, each of which receives only the information required to perform its function:

  • Cloudflare — website hosting, DNS, and content delivery. Cloudflare processes the technical details of each request, including your IP address, in order to serve the page and to protect the site from abuse.
  • Microsoft Clarity — website analytics and session replay. See Cookies & Analytics above.
  • Resend — email delivery. When you submit the contact form, the name, email address, and message you entered are passed to Resend so the message can be delivered to our inbox. Resend receives nothing about visitors who do not use the form.

6. Our Products

Byte Craft Software builds and operates several applications. Each holds its own data, under its own privacy policy, and nothing here grants this site access to any of it:

  • Dojo Companion — dojocompanion.com/privacy.html
  • Punchd — punchd.co/privacy.html
  • Long Rest — longrest.co/privacy.html

Visiting this site creates no account and gives us no access to any product data.

7. Retention & Your Rights

We keep messages you send us for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and to keep a record of our correspondence. Analytics data is retained by Microsoft Clarity according to their own retention schedule.

You may ask us what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, by emailing support@bytecraftsoftware.com. We will respond within 30 days, except where longer retention is required by law.

8. Children's Privacy

This site is aimed at businesses and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
